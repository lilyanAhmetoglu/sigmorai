## Goal

After this lesson you can:

- Check a brief against a model's limits (size, shape, length, speed) before you agree to it.
- Plan a longer video as several short shots.
- Read "preview", "deprecation" and "shut down", and plan for a model that goes away.

## The problem

A travel agency asks for two things by next week:

1. A 24-second landscape video of a hotel, in 4k, for the website.
2. 500 small square thumbnails for its hotel list.

The designer says "yes" in the meeting. Then she opens the video model's docs. The model makes clips of 8 seconds at most, and 4k works only at 8 seconds. She also finds that the model is marked **Preview**, and that another vendor recently shut down its video API.

She promised before she read the limits. Now she must re-plan under time pressure.

## The idea

Every media model has hard limits. Read them before you promise anything. Think of a camera lens: a great lens still has a fixed zoom range. You plan the shot around it.

This four-question check is Sigmorai's, not a vendor rule:

```text
1. What does it make?     image, video, audio
2. What sizes and length? resolution, shape, seconds
3. How fast?              seconds or minutes per result
4. How stable?            preview or stable? shutdown announced?
```

**Image models: one family, several jobs.** The [OpenAI docs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-generation) offer two GPT Image 2.5 models: "Choose Sunburst for workflows where editing precision matters most, and Flare for fast, high-quality everyday image generation."

- Recommended sizes: 1024x1024, 1536x1024 (landscape), 1024x1536 (portrait).
- Custom sizes: "Width and height must be multiples of 16, the aspect ratio must be between 1:3 and 3:1, and neither edge may exceed 3840 pixels." Also: "Resolutions above 2560x1440 are experimental."
- Speed: "Complex prompts may take up to 2 minutes to process."
- Weak spots: precise text placement; the model "may occasionally struggle to maintain visual consistency for recurring characters or brand elements across multiple generations"; and precise placement in layout-heavy designs.

The [Gemini API docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation) list several Nano Banana models:

- **Nano Banana 2.1**: the "primary high-efficiency workhorse model for image generation and conversational editing", at 1K, 2K and 4K.
- **Nano Banana Pro** (Gemini 3 Pro Image): "optimized for professional asset production".
- **Nano Banana 2 Lite**: "The fastest and cheapest Gemini image model". It "only supports 1K resolution" and is "Not optimized for multiple reference inputs".
- One more limit: "The model won't always follow the exact number of image outputs that the user explicitly asks for."

**Video models: short clips, strict rules.** The [Gemini API docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/veo) give these limits for Veo 3.1:

| Setting     | Veo 3.1                                                         |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Length      | 4, 6 or 8 seconds                                               |
| Resolution  | 720p, 1080p, 4k (1080p and 4k: 8 seconds only)                  |
| Shape       | 16:9 (default) or 9:16                                          |
| Frame rate  | 24fps                                                           |
| Per request | 1 video                                                         |
| Wait time   | "Min: 11 seconds; Max: 6 minutes (during peak hours)"           |
| Storage     | "stored on the server for 2 days, after which they are removed" |
| Status      | Preview                                                         |

Two more rules: you can extend a Veo video "by 7 seconds and up to 20 times", but "Video extension is also limited to 720p videos". And Veo 3.1 Lite has no 4k.

[Runway](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/models) offers its own video models, such as Gen-4.5 ("text to video and image to video"). Its model list also includes models from other companies, such as Veo 3.1. So one platform can give you several video models.

**Models get retired.**

- The [OpenAI docs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/video-generation) say: "The Sora 2 models and Videos API were shut down on September 24, 2026". Developers were told on March 24, 2026 ([OpenAI deprecations](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations)). "No one-to-one replacement API is available."
- The Gemini API docs list Imagen as "Google's legacy text-to-image models (shut down)".
- The [Claude docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/model-deprecations) say "Anthropic regularly retires older ones", and "Requests to retired models will fail."

The [Gemini deprecations page](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/deprecations) explains the words. A **deprecation** is "the announcement that we no longer provide support for a model, and that it will be 'shut down' in the near future". After shutdown, "it is completely turned off". Models are either **preview** or **stable**.

## Worked example

### Step 1: the brief against the limits (before)

```text
Brief:   24 s, 16:9, 4k, one video
Limits:  4k only at 8 s; extension only at 720p
Result:  one request can't do it. Extension can't keep 4k.
```

### Step 2: plan in shots (after)

A long video is usually several shots edited together. Plan three 8-second 4k shots:

```text
Shot 1 (8 s, 16:9, 4k): exterior at golden hour, slow push-in
Shot 2 (8 s, 16:9, 4k): lobby, camera glides past the desk
Shot 3 (8 s, 16:9, 4k): pool terrace, slow pan to the sea
Edit:   cut the three shots together = 24 s
```

**The hard case is the 4k length.** Extension looks like the easy path to 24 seconds. But the extend feature is 720p only, so it breaks the 4k need. Separate 8-second shots keep 4k.

### Step 3: plan the time and the files

```text
Each try:  11 s to 6 min wait
Storage:   2 days, then removed
Plan:      download every clip you keep on the same day
```

### Step 4: pick the image model for the thumbnails

500 small square thumbnails need speed and low cost, not 4K. Nano Banana 2 Lite fits: fast, cheap, 1K only. For a 4K hero image, Lite is the wrong pick.

### Step 5: plan for change

The video model is in Preview, and another vendor just shut down its video API. Keep the shot list above in your own files. If the model is retired, you can try the same shot descriptions on another video model.

### Step 6: why it is better

- **The promise matches the limits.** You know the plan works before the client hears it.
- **Quality is kept.** Three 4k shots, not one stretched 720p clip.
- **Nothing is lost.** Clips are saved before the 2-day limit.
- **The work can move.** Written shot lists don't depend on one model.

## Common mistakes

1. **Saying yes before reading the limits.** Fix: run the four-question check on the docs before the meeting ends.
2. **Using extension for a high-resolution video.** Extension is 720p only. Fix: plan separate 8-second shots at 1080p or 4k, and cut them together.
3. **One model, no backup.** Models get deprecated and shut down. Fix: store prompts and shot lists where you control them, and read the vendor's deprecation page.

## Try it

Not graded. Write your answer down before you read the answer.

A teammate plans three requests. For each one, say if it fits the limits above. If not, say what to change.

```text
1. GPT Image 2.5 Flare: a 4000x1000 web banner.
2. Veo 3.1 Lite: an 8-second clip in 4k.
3. Nano Banana 2 Lite: 50 product shots, each one
   mixing 6 reference photos.
```

### Answer

1. **Does not fit.** The edge is over 3840 pixels, and 4:1 is wider than the 3:1 limit. Ask for a 3:1 size with sides that are multiples of 16, such as 3072x1024. Sizes above 2560x1440 are experimental, so test first. Or crop the 4:1 shape in your design tool.
2. **Does not fit.** Veo 3.1 Lite has no 4k. Use Veo 3.1 (or Veo 3.1 Fast) at 4k, 8 seconds.
3. **Risky.** Nano Banana 2 Lite is "Not optimized for multiple reference inputs". Use Nano Banana 2.1 for shots that mix many references.

## Recap

- Read the limits (size, shape, length, speed, status) before you agree to a brief.
- Short clip limits mean a longer video is several shots edited together. Extension works only at 720p.
- Models are deprecated and shut down. Keep your shot lists portable, and download results quickly.
